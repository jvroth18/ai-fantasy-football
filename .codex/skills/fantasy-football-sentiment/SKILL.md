# Fantasy Football Sentiment

Build a dated view of what analysts and fantasy managers currently believe about players.

## Workflow

- Browse current sources whenever the requested answer could affect a draft, waiver, lineup, or trade decision.
- Separate analyst thesis, observable market movement such as ADP, and informal community sentiment.
- Prefer several independent analysts plus a quantitative market source. Treat camp reports and highlights as noisy until supported by role, usage, or depth-chart evidence.
- State league format and time horizon. Do not merge redraft, dynasty, best ball, superflex, or tight-end-premium conclusions.
- Record publication or observation dates and link directly to sources.
- Label synthesis and inference clearly. Never present popularity as a projection or recommendation by itself.
